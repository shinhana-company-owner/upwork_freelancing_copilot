# UPWORK AGENT

Official Upwork MCP: https://mcp.upwork.com/mcp
Published: October 2nd, 2026

The agent that connect to recent published MCP from Upwork to retrieve some features

## Stop wasting Connects on low-quality or ghost jobs (biggest win)
**Problem**: Spending $50–200/month on Connects for jobs that never hire or never reply.

**MCP help**:
- Ask your AI agent to search jobs and score them before you spend any Connects.
- Useful prompts:
    - “Search for [your niche] jobs posted in the last 2 hours with budget over $X and fewer than 15 proposals. Rank them by client spend, hire rate, and payment verification.”
    - “For each of these jobs, pull full details and tell me: number of proposals, client hire rate, total spent, member since, and whether payment is verified. Flag any that look like ghost/scam posts.”

- The agent can draft the proposal only for the highest-quality ones, so you review and confirm instead of applying blindly.

This alone can cut Connects waste dramatically.

## Faster, higher-quality proposals with less effort
**Problem**: Writing good proposals is time-consuming; generic ones get ignored.

**MCP help**:
- “Draft a proposal for this job using my profile, past relevant work, and the exact requirements. Keep it under 200 words, open with the client’s problem, and end with one clear question.”
- The MCP can pull your profile, past contracts, and the job details in one conversation so the draft is grounded in real data.
- You still review and approve (draft-and-confirm flow), so you stay in control while spending far less time.

## Monitor your proposals and client activity without living on the site
**Problem**: You apply and then have no idea if the client even opened the proposal.

**MCP help** (especially useful if you have Freelancer Plus for insights):
- “Show me all my active proposals, how many have been viewed/opened/shortlisted, and which ones need follow-up.”
- “Summarize new invitations, offers, and messages from the last 24 hours.”
Set a recurring prompt (daily or every few hours) so the agent checks for you.

This reduces the constant “refresh the proposals page” anxiety.

## Protect your Job Success Score and reputation
**Problem**: One bad client or unfair review can tank your JSS.

**MCP help**:
- Before accepting or continuing a contract: “Pull the client’s history — number of hires, spend, feedback given to other freelancers, and any red flags.”
- After a contract ends: “Summarize this contract and draft a polite message asking for feedback if they haven’t left any yet.”
- Keep a running view of your active contracts and milestones so you catch problems early.

## Better visibility into your own account health
**Problem**: Surprises with Connects balance, earnings, or contract status.

**MCP help**:
- One-call overview: “Give me a dashboard of my Connects balance, active proposals, invitations, messages, and open contracts.”
- Track earnings and timesheets without logging into the website constantly.
Quickly check if a job you applied to is still open or has been filled.

## Reduce scam / low-intent job exposure
**Problem**: Fake or unserious posts that waste Connects and time.

**MCP help**:
- Instruct the agent with strict filters: only payment-verified clients, hire rate above X%, total spent above $Y, jobs posted recently, and clear requirements.
- “Exclude any job that looks like a test post, has no description detail, or has extremely low budget for the scope.”
